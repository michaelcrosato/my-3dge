Add a setting to the playground: `playground.gym.gravity`, the gravity the player falls under in the gym.

- In m/s², Apex's 19.05 by default, from 1 to 50; read when the scene starts, so it changes play and goes in replays,
  and `node x set`, URL parameters and `__engine.set` reach it, as the gym's other settings.
- Under any gravity the player still jumps `playground.gym.jump` high: under less he rises and falls slower, longer
  in the air.
- At its default the gym plays exactly as before: its replays keep their golden hashes.
- The playground's README lists it with the gym's other settings.
